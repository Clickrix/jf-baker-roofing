Columbus has trusted the Baker family since 1929.

JF Baker Roofing Company was founded in 1929 by James Franklin Baker and has been serving Columbus ever since — through three generations of the same family. From historical roof replacements to custom sheet metal fabrication, we've done it all.

We serve Columbus and surrounding Central Ohio communities within a 50-mile radius, including Dublin, Upper Arlington, Delaware, Westerville, Gahanna, Hilliard, Powell, and Worthington. Fully licensed, bonded, and insured — and every project starts with a free, no-obligation estimate.
